How It Works

1
Name your fund — anything from "The [Family] Fund" to a legacy theme.
2
Make an initial contribution — cash, securities, or other assets ($10,000 minimum).
3
Your gift is invested — growing tax-free until you're ready to grant.
4
Recommend grants — anytime, to any eligible nonprofit you choose.
